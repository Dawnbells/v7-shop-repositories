V7 Order Connector

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how we collect, use, retain and delete data when a merchant installs the Shopify app V7 Order Connector (the "App"). The App only reads order and product data, and its sole purpose is to sync the merchant's Shopify orders into the merchant's own order management back office.

For the personal information of store customers, the merchant is the data controller; we process that data only on the merchant's behalf and according to the merchant's instructions.

1. Data we collect

We only read data within the access scopes the merchant grants (read_orders and read_products):

  • Order information: order number, time, payment, fulfillment and cancellation status, amounts, discounts, shipping, taxes, notes and tags.
  • Recipient information: name, phone number, email address and shipping / billing address.
  • Customer technical information: the customer's IP address and browser user agent at checkout.
  • Product information: product name, variant, SKU, quantity, price and related product metafields.
  • Store information: store domain, access token, granted scopes, install and uninstall time.

We do not collect data directly from customers, and the App does not add cookies or scripts to the storefront.

2. How we use the data

We use the data only to provide the App's service to the merchant: reviewing orders, fulfilling and shipping them, printing shipping labels, and identifying high-risk orders. We do not use it for advertising, marketing or profiling.

3. Data sharing

  • We do not sell data obtained from Shopify, and do not share it with third parties for their own use.
  • The data is only available inside the merchant's own account, to team members the merchant has authorized.
  • We may disclose data where required by law, to the extent necessary.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Recipient personal information is automatically redacted 12 months after the order was last updated. Non-personal order data is kept for reconciliation and reporting.
  • When the App is uninstalled, syncing stops and the store's access token is deleted.
  • We handle Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks within 30 days: shop/redact (redact all personal data of the store's orders and delete its credentials), customers/redact (redact that customer's orders) and customers/data_request (export that customer's order data to the merchant).

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Every Shopify webhook and OAuth callback is verified with an HMAC signature. Access tokens are stored only on the server and never shown in the interface or written to logs. Access to the back office is permission-controlled, and viewing or exporting recipient information is logged.

6. Your rights

Customers may have rights to access, correct or delete their personal information (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA). Please send such requests to the store you ordered from; when Shopify forwards a request to us, we handle it as described in section 4.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
